About AgriConnectX

AgriConnectX is a digital platform designed to revolutionize agriculture in Cameroon by bridging the gap between farmers and buyers.

Our mission is to empower local farmers with tools and technology to sell their produce directly, ensuring fair prices and sustainable livelihoods.

  • Support over 10,000 farmers across Cameroon
  • Enable transparent pricing and fair trade
  • Provide agricultural education and market information
  • Build a sustainable farming community
